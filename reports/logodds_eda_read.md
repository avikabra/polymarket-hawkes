# Log-Odds EDA — Company Contracts (real data)

> **Real company bars.** 594,984 Data-API trades across 6,486 markets → resampled to 1-minute
> log-odds bars. Figures computed on the **liquid subset** (volume > \$10k = 1,452 markets,
> **72,500,135** 1-minute bars). Figures: `reports/figures/*_company.png`. Screen:
> `data/polymarket/liquidity_screen.parquet`. Generated by `scripts/eda_logodds.py --label company`
> + `scratch/liquidity_and_reaction_stats.py`.

**One-line read: a real Polymarket reaction is mostly flat, punctuated by large jumps — the signal
is real but sparse, so the reaction target must not be a naive fixed-clock last-trade Δ.**

## Figure 1 — 6h log-odds change distribution
`y_logit_Δ = close_lo[t+Δ] − close_lo[t]`, Δ = 6h, n = 72.0M. A dominant spike at exactly zero,
mean ≈ 0, **std = 0.44**, fat symmetric tails. The zero spike is LOCF between sparse trades, not
genuine "no news."

## Figure 2 — Zero-move share (EPS = 0.01 log-odds)
**74.6% of 6h windows are zero-move**, even on the liquid subset; per-contract median = 64.8%.
Direct confirmation that inactivity manufactures zeros. A naive 6h last-trade Δ is ~3/4 structural
zeros — target selection must account for this (shorten Δ, or condition on trades/events).

## Figure 3 — Trade frequency & volume by contract
Per-contract medians: **154 trades over 14 active trading days**. Trade-level liquidity screen
(liquid subset): ≥50 trades 80%, ≥200 trades 40%, ≥10 active days 58%. A defensible "clean" set —
**≥200 trades AND ≥10 active days — is 473 contracts (32.6%)**.

## Figure 4 — Demean + normalize
Raw vs demeaned/unit-variance log-odds for the 4 most-active contracts. Normalization removes
cross-contract level differences (different strikes/regimes); the standardized series is the form
the model should consume.

## Figure 5 — Event-time plot
**Synthetic events only** (one per contract at series midpoint, seed=42) — validates the event-time
alignment machinery. Real event-time reactions require the article-matching pipeline (Q3); no causal
signal should be read from the synthetic version.

## When they move, they move
Among non-zero 6h windows: median |Δ| = **0.41 log-odds**, 90th pct = 1.66, 99th pct = 5.17. The
reaction is economically large when it happens — the challenge is sparsity, not absence.

## Implication for the target (not decided here)
Options: (1) shorter Δ (1–2h); (2) **event-/trade-conditioned windows** rather than a fixed clock
grid — most promising given 75% structural zeros; (3) restrict to the clean 473-contract set. The
target choice is deferred to Prof. Kelly per instruction.
