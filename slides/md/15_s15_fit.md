<div class="row r1"><div class="sn">1</div>
<div class="lt"><div class="tt">Weights</div><div class="dd">The weights <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mrow><mi>b</mi></mrow></math></span> turn each company's news shock into a bet on its reaction on day <span style="white-space:nowrap"><span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mrow><mi>d</mi></mrow></math></span>.</span> A positive bet gains when the reaction is positive and loses when it is negative.</div></div>
<div class="rt"><div class="e"><span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mrow><msub><mi>R</mi><mi>d</mi></msub><mo>=</mo><msub><mo>∑</mo><mi>k</mi></msub><msub><mi>ξ</mi><mrow><mi>k</mi><mo separator="true">,</mo><mi>d</mi></mrow></msub><msub><mo>∑</mo><mi>j</mi></msub><msub><mi>b</mi><mi>j</mi></msub><mtext> </mtext><msub><mi>u</mi><mrow><mi>k</mi><mo separator="true">,</mo><mi>d</mi><mo separator="true">,</mo><mi>j</mi></mrow></msub></mrow></math></span></div><div class="n"><span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mrow><msub><mi>b</mi><mi>j</mi></msub></mrow></math></span> is the weight on number <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mrow><mi>j</mi></mrow></math></span> of the news shock <span style="white-space:nowrap"><span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mrow><msub><mi>u</mi><mrow><mi>k</mi><mo separator="true">,</mo><mi>d</mi></mrow></msub></mrow></math></span>.</span> The inner sum is the bet on company <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mrow><mi>k</mi></mrow></math></span> that arises from its news shock, and <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mrow><msub><mi>ξ</mi><mrow><mi>k</mi><mo separator="true">,</mo><mi>d</mi></mrow></msub></mrow></math></span> is its standardized reaction. <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mrow><msub><mi>R</mi><mi>d</mi></msub></mrow></math></span> is the return of day <span style="white-space:nowrap"><span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mrow><mi>d</mi></mrow></math></span>.</span></div></div></div>

<div class="row r2"><div class="sn">2</div>
<div class="lt"><div class="tt">Choosing the weights</div><div class="dd">This is maximum Sharpe ratio regression (MSRR). We choose <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mrow><mi>b</mi></mrow></math></span> so that the return <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mrow><msub><mi>R</mi><mi>d</mi></msub></mrow></math></span> is close to 1 on every training day. A high and steady return is a high Sharpe ratio. A penalty on large weights keeps <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mrow><mi>b</mi></mrow></math></span> from fitting noise.</div></div>

The rows of <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mrow><mi>F</mi></mrow></math></span> are the <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mrow><msub><mi>F</mi><mi>d</mi></msub></mrow></math></span> of the training days. <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mrow><mn mathvariant="bold">1</mn></mrow></math></span> is a vector of ones, <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mrow><mi>I</mi></mrow></math></span> is the identity, and <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mrow><mi>λ</mi></mrow></math></span> is the penalty. <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mrow><mover accent="true"><mi>b</mi><mo>^</mo></mover></mrow></math></span> is the <span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mrow><mi>b</mi></mrow></math></span> that minimizes <span style="white-space:nowrap"><span class="katex"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mrow><msub><mo>∑</mo><mi>d</mi></msub><mo stretchy="false">(</mo><mn>1</mn><mo>−</mo><mi>b</mi><mo>⋅</mo><msub><mi>F</mi><mi>d</mi></msub><msup><mo stretchy="false">)</mo><mn>2</mn></msup><mo>+</mo><mi>λ</mi><mtext> </mtext><mi>b</mi><mo>⋅</mo><mi>b</mi></mrow></math></span>,</span> which penalizes returns far from 1, high or low.</div></div></div>
